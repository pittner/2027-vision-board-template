Fill it in 30 minutes

  1. Write 2027 in one word first. If you cannot, the rest of the sheet will be a wish list.
  2. Write the one sentence that must be true by 31 December 2027.
  3. Give each quarter one picture and one proof you can check on its date: 31 March, 30 June, 30 September, 31 December.
  4. Name what you leave in 2026 and what you keep doing. Both lines matter.
  5. Do the first step before 1 January and write the date next to it.
  6. Tick a review box on each quarter date. Change the picture if the goal changed, not the date.

Can I get the 2027 template as a PDF?

Press Print and choose Save as PDF in the print dialog. That gives a vector PDF at the exact paper size, sharper than a hosted PDF.

When should I make my 2027 vision board?

Before January, ideally in October or November. A board made in autumn has a first step that is already done on 1 January, a board made on 31 December usually has none.
